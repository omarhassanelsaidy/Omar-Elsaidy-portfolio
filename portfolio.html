Case study

Building the funnel behind KAITECH's BIM & AI scholarship

Every piece of content was written to move one specific audience — recent engineering grads, working engineers, senior specialists — one step closer to enrolling. Below is the actual funnel logic used.

Awareness

Speak to the fear, not the feature

Posts opened with the question the audience was already asking themselves — "final year and still don't feel job-ready?" — before introducing the diploma.

"The certificate alone isn't enough anymore — companies want people who can already work the tools real projects use."

Consideration

Show the roadmap, not the syllabus

Carousels broke the diploma into a visible path — BIM fundamentals → AI & prompt engineering → automation with Dynamo/Python → a capstone project — so prospects could see exactly where they'd end up.

"Every stage builds on the one before it, so you're market-ready, not just certificate-ready."

Sales

Let graduates close the deal

Reels cut between real graduates answering the same question in their own words — proof over promises — paired with a direct link/WhatsApp CTA during enrollment windows.

"This could be your success story next. Book your seat through the link."
